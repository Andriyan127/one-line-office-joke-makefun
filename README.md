## The entry

> When my boss called us 'a family,' I knew the layoffs were coming.

66 characters, one sentence, one joke.

## Why this line

1. **One-line office joke** — a single sentence that is a complete joke about office life: the boss, the team, the layoff. Not a caption or stray observation.
2. **Funny** — the setup is the most familiar corporate pep-talk line; the punchline flips it into the thing every office worker dreads, and it lands because the "family" speech always seems to precede bad news.
3. **One line** — setup and punchline on a single line, no rambling, 66 characters.
4. **Fresh** — built on a specific office detail (the "we're a family" speech) rather than a generic complaint about Mondays or meetings.
5. **Clear on first read** — plain words, no reference the reader cannot follow, no explanation needed.
